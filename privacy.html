rclone-access privacy policy

rclone-access is a personal configuration of rclone used by one person, the owner, with their own Google account.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, rclone accesses the owner's Google Drive files and metadata through the Google Drive API.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens stay on the owner's own computers. Files are used only by the owner and the tools the owner chooses to run, which can include AI assistants. This site collects no data, and no data is sold.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time in their Google Account permissions.
